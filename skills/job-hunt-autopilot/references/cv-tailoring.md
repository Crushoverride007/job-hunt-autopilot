# CV tailoring without fabrication

Inspired by the anti-fabrication ideas in claude-resume-kit (MIT), simplified for job seekers
without LaTeX.

## When to tailor
- Default: pick the closest pre-made version (e.g. Azure vs AWS). Uploading a new file per job
  is slow and only possible in Desktop/Cowork.
- Tailor a one-off version only for top matches (high fit + high pay, or a recruiter who
  reached out). Name it `<Company>-<Name>_CV.pdf`.

## What you may change
- Headline and summary emphasis (lead with what the job asks for).
- Order of roles' bullets and of skills (most relevant first).
- Wording to mirror the job's terms **when they mean the same thing**
  (e.g. "CI/CD security gates" <-> "pipeline security scanning").
- Remove less relevant bullets to keep one page.

## What you must never change
- Add a tool, cert, employer, title, degree or number that isn't in the source CV or confirmed
  by the user.
- Upgrade verbs beyond reality ("contributed to" -> "led", "used" -> "architected").
- Change dates, years of experience, or work-authorisation facts.
- Claim production experience for something the user only knows in theory.

## Quality checks before saving
1. Every claim traces to the source CV or a user confirmation.
2. One page, single column, real text (no text in images), standard section names.
3. Contact line uses the answer-bank email.
4. No AI-sounding filler: passionate, leverage, spearheaded, synergy, cutting-edge,
   results-driven, dynamic, "I am excited to".
5. Read it as: ATS bot (keywords present?), recruiter (clear in 10 seconds?), hiring manager
   (concrete numbers?).
